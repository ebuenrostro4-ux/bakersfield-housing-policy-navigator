TRAINING EXAMPLE ONLY - NOT A LAW OR CITY POLICY
Policy identifier: DEMO-PERMIT-002
Title: Housing Application Coordination Practice
Status: Fictional training material; no legislative status is asserted.

Section 1. Scope
This fictional practice applies only to a simulated housing application used in a classroom demonstration.

Section 2. Completeness record
For each simulated housing application, staff shall prepare a written completeness checklist and retain the checklist with the application record.

Section 3. Department coordination
Staff shall record referral to Planning and Building & Safety and identify a staff owner for each simulated housing application.

Section 4. Timing
No effective date or statutory deadline is established by this training example.

This text has no legal effect. It does not describe a current California housing requirement or an adopted City of Bakersfield procedure.
